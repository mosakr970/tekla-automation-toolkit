Every EK-8xx summary - foundations, columns and beams, wall panels, slabs, stairs, timber, steel assemblies and their parts, rebar and bolts - written to its own Excel template in one run, from the model's own Organizer presets. The model's assemblies are grouped by prefix, each prefix goes into its report, and every report that is ticked comes out as a finished workbook.

A

What it does

It is a modeling macro, run from the model. It reads the reports the office already keeps for Organizer - the EK-8xx_*.propertytemplate presets and the 23XX_*_EK-8-8xx_*.xltx Excel templates in the model's ProjectOrganizerData folder - and pairs them by number. Archived presets in 00_arhiiv are left out.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Nothing is read until Scan is pressed. With assemblies selected in the model only those are scanned, otherwise the whole model. Every top-level assembly and cast unit is grouped by its exact numbering prefix, so TD and TD- are two rows.

  2. 02

    Check the list

    Every report is listed with the prefixes that go into it, and the count of elements and positions. A prefix goes where its own entry in the office's prefix table says, otherwise where the longest entry it starts with says, so LV_A- goes with LV. Right-click a prefix and choose Move to to put it in another report. Untick a report or a prefix to leave it out.

  3. 03

    Export

    Every ticked report is written to its Excel template in the model's Reports folder, named after the template with the project number, the revision and the date. Enter on any line selects its elements in the model first, to see what will be listed - Shift or Ctrl picks several lines.

Where prefixes go is the office's, the ticks are the project's

The prefix table is kept beside the macro in ek_reports.json and is the same in every model. Save writes a moved prefix to it. The ticks and the Designer and Revision fields belong to the project and are kept in the model, in EKReports.settings.

C

From the preset to the row

The preset gives the columns, in order: the properties tried in turn until one has a value (EK_MARK, then ASSEMBLY_POS), the formulas Organizer calculates, and the units. The Excel template gives only the look: the header, its fields, and the %&O%& cell the table starts at, whose style every table cell takes.

  • Identical pieces share a row. Every text the same, every length and level within 1 mm, every area, volume and weight within 0.1 % - or 0.01 kg for small parts. The count goes in Arv.
  • Area, volume and weight are one piece's. The total row, Kokku, adds them up over every piece. A kokku column, such as Kaal kokku, shows the whole row. Lengths are never totalled.
  • The decimals are the office's. None in mm, 3 for levels in m, 2 for any other m, m², m³, t or kg - whatever the preset says.
  • The header is filled in. The PROJECT.* fields from the project, the date and revision from the window, and PROJEKTEERIJA from the Designer field. The revision also replaces the v01 of the document number, EK-8-801_v01.
D

The traps

Each of these made a report that was slightly wrong, without an error. All are handled now.

  • A cast unit's VOLUME_NET counts its steel embeds. EK-803's insulation volume is VOLUME_NET less the concrete, so the anchors came out as insulation. The macro adds up the cast unit's own parts instead.
  • CUSTOM.INSULATION_THICKNESS is 0 on every sandwich panel. The thickness is read from the insulation part itself.
  • MODEL_TOTAL counts a bolt's twins across the whole model. Added up over a row it counted them many times over, so a row counts the bolt group's own bolts.
  • Tekla can list a sub-assembly twice. Its parts were counted twice in the parts reports. Each part is now listed once.
  • Pieces with no mark merged with different weights. EK-851's total came out 33 kg short. The weights are now compared, and the totals add up every piece.
Templates

A template of the usual shape - the count second - takes the columns in order. Any other, such as EK-821 with its two-row head or EK-862 without a count, is matched by column head. If a column is added to a preset in Organizer, add its head to the Excel template too.

F

Limitations

  • Unnumbered pieces show (?) workflow

    A position like SWP-000(?) means the numbering is out of date. Number the model first.

  • EK-851 needs EK_MARK workflow

    Its first column has no fallback, so run EK Mark on the steel first.

  • The rebar prefixes are fixed known

    The cast-in-place prefixes of EK-821 are in the macro, not in the prefix table.

  • Insulation is told apart by material type known

    A panel with another MISCELLANEOUS part, such as a bearing pad, would count it as insulation.

H

Running it

  1. Install the macro with Install-All.bat or by double-clicking EKReports_1.0.tsep from config/release/, then restart Tekla. It is in Esplan tools in Applications & components.
  2. Select assemblies to report only those, or nothing for the whole model, and press Scan.
  3. Tick the reports and prefixes, fill in Designer, Revision and Date, and press Export.
  4. Open folder shows the workbooks in the model's Reports folder.
