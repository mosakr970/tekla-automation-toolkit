How each tool is built

One file, no install

Tekla compiles a .cs macro at startup. Handing one to a colleague is sending a file.

The measuring is the hard part

The rules are simple once the number is trustworthy. Getting it out of Tekla's geometry is where the time goes.

Verify before trusting

Cross-checks against a second implementation, tested assumptions, and probe macros.

Limits stated

Every case study separates measured results from estimates and lists what the tool does not handle.
